Add a claim to check

Give a claimed place and time, and this computes exactly where the sun really was — so you can compare it against what the shadows and lighting actually look like in the photo. This runs entirely offline, no AI involved, just real astronomy.

Use UTC, not local time — convert first if you know the timezone. Coordinates from a Landmark Detection result on an Evidence page can be pasted straight in here.
